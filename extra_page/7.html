IC Workshop Seminar

January 15, 2023 - January 19, 2023

During this three-day Power and Energy Engineering Association seminar, I gained an in-depth understanding of Taiwan's power grid layout and relevant regulations. This experience not only expanded my professional knowledge but also provided me with a deeper insight into future energy policies.

The main contents of the seminar included:

  • Current status and future planning of Taiwan's power system
  • Renewable energy development and its application in the power grid
  • Smart grid technology and challenges
  • Analysis of energy regulations and policies

Through communication with industry experts, I further strengthened my resolve to develop in the field of power engineering and gained a clearer understanding of how to apply the knowledge acquired to practical projects.
